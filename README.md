# AFK Power Saver

AFK Power Saver is a Windows utility for saving power when you are AFK while gaming. Say you're building something in a survival game and are just waiting for 5 minutes, blasting your GPU for basically no reason... Just hit the hotkey and boom, you just saved a ton of power, heat and noise. Once you're ready to actively game, hit the hotkey again to restore default power settings.

You can also limit CPU usage and dim the screen to save monitor power too. I probably wouldn't bother with the CPU limit but it's there for completeness.

There's also an idle activation timer. Say your oven goes off and you need to go attend to your food: after the preset delay, the app kicks in and limits your power usage. When you get back and move your mouse or use the keyboard, it auto-restores! Super convenient, right...

I hope you find the app useful, I certainly have. If you save a little power and money, please consider throwing me a little something. Donation links in the app.
